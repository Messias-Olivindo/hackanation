# Hackanation 2026

Centralized info about the hackathon this repo participates in.

- **Official site**: https://tokennation.com.br/hackanation/

## Overview

- **Name**: Hackanation 2026 (3rd edition)
- **Organizer**: TokenNation
- **Co-hosts / Partners**: Solana, Superteam Brasil, Chainlink Labs, Inteli, FIAP, TAIKAI, Tether (USDT sponsor)
- **Format**: Hybrid (online + in-person)
- **Theme**: Blockchain, entrepreneurship, social impact
- **Industries**: Blockchain, Web3, AI
- **Registered participants**: ~150

## Key Dates

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| Mar 2026 | Community warm-up, Twitter Spaces |
| Apr 24, 2026 | Bootcamp at Inteli — hackathon kickoff |

| May 31, 2026 23:00 | Initial submission deadline |

## Tracks

### 1. Solana Ecosystem — "Real World Web3 Applications"

Three categories:

1. **Payments, RWAs & Tokenization** — payment systems with stablecoins, credit/ticket/asset tokenization, fintech integrations.
2. **DeFi & New Financial Models** — decentralized financial products, novel credit mechanisms, prediction markets, sustainable protocols.
3. **Infrastructure & Dev Tools** — SDKs, APIs, debugging tools, Solana dev tooling.

Sponsor: Superteam Brasil (official Solana builder community in Brazil) + Tether.

## Prizes

- **Total pool**: R$ 30,000+
- **Solana / Superteam Brasil track**: R$ 25,000 (paid in USDT) — sources also cite "US$ 5,000 in USDT" for the Solana track specifically
